**FY2027 Outreach Plan**

This memo summarizes the **outreach plan** for Anytown and the surrounding counties. Questions go to the *Public Contact Team*.

- Quarterly town halls
  - January and April at the office
  - July and October at the county libraries
- Monthly claims clinics

Steps to request a clinic:

1. Email the Public Contact Team
2. Confirm the venue two weeks ahead
