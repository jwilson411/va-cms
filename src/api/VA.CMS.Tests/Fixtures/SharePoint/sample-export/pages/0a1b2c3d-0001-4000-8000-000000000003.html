A disability compensation claim moves through eight tracked steps. Each step is described below.

  1. Claim received – VA has received the claim.
  2. Under review – a Veterans Service Representative checks for required evidence.
  3. Gathering of evidence – VA requests records and schedules exams.
  4. Review of evidence
  5. Preparation for decision
  6. Pending decision approval
  7. Preparation for notification
  8. Complete – the decision letter is mailed.

Download the VA Form 21-526EZ instructions before filing. Legacy help is on the SharePoint help page.
